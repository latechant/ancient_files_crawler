The text above incorporates content created automatically using artifical intelligence.Source: "Jinhua Huang Xiansheng Wenyi" (金华黄先生文集) is a literary collection comprising twenty-three volumes, authored by Huang Jin during the Yuan Dynasty. The compilation and editing are credited to Lincan Wei Su and Panyang Liu Er.

According to Zhang's "Catalogue of Hidden Books," which cites the "Gong Wanzhai Collection," the original work consisted of forty-three volumes. However, Qian Qianyi's postscript in his "Qianyan Tang Ji" mentions that the initial draft was three volumes, with subsequent additions totaling thirty volumes. The extant versions include volumes one to thirteen and twenty-two to thirty-one, which are more extensive than the twenty-three-volume Yuan Dynasty edition. This version is believed to be the same as the one held by Shilijiu, as noted by Qian Qianyi.

The collection features a preface written by Gong Shitai in the 15th year of the Zhiyuan era (1355), when the original documents were still available. Gong obtained his copy from Wang Zhongwen and had it printed at the San Shan Academy, though such editions are now exceedingly rare. Efforts to supplement the missing volumes have been unsuccessful, which is a matter of regret.

The collection includes Qian Qianyi's handwritten postscript, which can be found in both "Qianyan Tang Ji" and "Airi Jinglu Zangshu Zhi." The work has also been included in the "Sibu Congkan Chubian," published by the Commercial Press.
