 ## 白话文翻译

该经文主要描述古代风水占卜中的住宅选址与建筑布局规则，涉及方位、水流、地形等要素对居住者吉凶的影响。例如：

- **北方**：若有污水池或未开垦的沼泽，不利财富；若有流水通达江河则为吉。
- **南方**：有林木、高丘则富贵；若水流停滞则凶。
- **东西方位**：东有流水、南有迂回池塘为吉；西有直流水或枯池则凶。
- **建筑布局**：强调内外院协调，门向、井灶位置需符合天道月德，违者易招病灾、官讼。
- **特殊地形**：如“天柱地”（内高四方低）主富贵，“非位地”（独高无依）主贫贱。
- **吉凶判断**：通过称量土壤重量（如三两凶、七两吉）及步数测量（如百步为“开”吉，八十步为“危”凶）来定宅地优劣。

 ##Vernacular Translation

This scripture mainly describes the rules for residential site selection and building layout in ancient feng shui divination, involving the influence of factors such as direction, water flow, and terrain on the good fortune or bad fortune of the residents. For example:

- ** North **: If there are sewage ponds or unreclaimed swamps, it will be unfavorable for wealth; if there is running water to reach rivers, it will be good.
- ** South **: If there are trees and high hills, wealth will be achieved; if the water flow stops, it will be fierce.
- ** East-west direction **: Running water in the east and winding ponds in the south are auspicious; straight running water or dead ponds in the west are dangerous.
- ** Architectural layout **: Emphasize the coordination of internal and external courtyards. The direction of the door and the location of the well and stove must comply with the laws of heaven and the moon. Violators are prone to illness, disaster and official litigation.
- ** Special terrain **: For example,"Tianzhu Land"(high inside and low in all directions) dominates wealth, and "non-position land"(only high and no support) dominates poverty and low.
- ** Judgment of good or bad luck **: Determine the advantages and disadvantages of the homestead by weighing the soil (such as three or two bad luck, seven or two good luck) and measuring the number of steps (such as a hundred steps for "opening" auspicious, and eighty steps for "dangerous" auspicious).

### 3. **文化内涵**
- **数术传统**：体现古代“相地术”与“择日法”，如“九宫八卦”布局、“天道月德”择吉日动土。

- 与《葬书》《地理新书》等典籍一脉相承，是研究古代建筑风水、民俗信仰的重要资料。

 ##Related background

### 1. ** Nature of the document **
- This text belongs to an ancient feng shui divination document, which may originate from the "Zhai Jing" classics from the Han Dynasty to the Tang Dynasty. The content is similar to the "Huang Di Zhai Jing" and reflects the ancient concept of living inspired by heaven and man.

### 2. ** Core ideas **
- ** Location: Good and bad luck **: Based on the five elements of yin and yang, combining the four elephants of Qinglong, white tiger, rosefinch and Xuanwu, it emphasizes the corresponding relationship between residential location and the natural environment.
- ** Terrain selection **: Judging good or bad luck through the direction of water flow, terrain height, and soil properties. For example,"flowing water leads to rivers" is the source of wealth, and "dry ponds" are the main source of decline.
- ** Architectural taboos **: Doors, wells, stoves, warehouses and other locations must comply with the laws of heaven and moon, and violators will cause disasters such as illness, imprisonment, and thieves.

### 3. ** Cultural connotation **
- ** Tradition of mathematics **: Reflecting the ancient "physiognomy" and "day selection method", such as the layout of the "Nine Palaces and Eight Trigrams" and the "Heaven and Moon Virtue" choosing an auspicious day to start the earth.
- ** Social concept **: Reflecting the ancient importance attached to the destiny, wealth and status of the family, using feng shui to avoid risks and pursue "long-term retention of the position."

### 4. ** Historical connection **
- It is in line with classics such as "Burial Book" and "New Geography Book", and is an important material for studying ancient architectural feng shui and folk beliefs.
- Specific operations such as "land measurement method" and "step number method" in the article confirm the practical tradition of "measuring land to make a city" in "Zhou Li·Kaogongji".

### 5. ** Modern value **
- Provide cases for ancient environmental science and architectural planning research, and demonstrate the ancestors 'observation and utilization wisdom of the natural environment.
- Some terms (such as "Tianzhu Land" and "Longbei") are still found in contemporary feng shui theory, which shows the continuity of culture.
