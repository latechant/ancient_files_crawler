                    16世纪中后期帕竹第悉相关研究问题考述

                    摘要：
                    目前学界有关帕竹第悉的研究，主要集中于14至16世纪前期，对16世纪中后期的帕竹第悉，不仅着墨较少，且疑点颇多。文章在充分借鉴国内外相关研究成果的基础上，综合运用藏汉文史料，尤其是以往的研究中鲜有运用的《噶玛噶仓传承大宝传记·无边宝月水晶鬘》和《白玛噶波文集》中的相关记载，在厘清阿旺扎西扎巴和卓微衮波二人卒年的基础上，对阿旺扎西扎巴的继任者，以及见载于明代汉文史籍中的阐化王札释藏卜和札思巴札失坚参二人的身份进行考证。

                    
                        关键词：
                            16世纪中后期;
                            帕竹第悉;
                            札释藏卜;
                            札思巴札失坚参;

                Abstract：
                At present, the research on Phag gru sde srid mainly focuses on the 14th century to the early 16th century, and the middle and late 16th century Phag gru sde srid is not only rarely discussed, but also has many doubts. Based on the relevant research results at home and abroad, this paper comprehensively uses Xizang and Chinese historical materials, especially the relevant records in the sgrub brgyud karma kam tshang gib rgyud pa rin po chevi rnam par thar pa rab vbyams nor bu zla ba chu shel gyi phreng ba and The collection ofp adma dkar po, which are rarely used in previous studies. On the basis of clarifying the dead time of ngag dbang bkra shis grags pa and vgro bavi mgon po, the successor of ngag dbang bkra shis grags pa and the identity of the two chan Kings, bkra shis bzang po and grags pa bkra shis rgyal mtshan, which are described in the Chinese historical records of Ming Dynasty.

                  KeyWords：
                        mid to late 16th century;
                        Phag gru sde srid;
                        Bkra shis bzang po;
                        Grags pa bkra shis rgyal mtshan;
