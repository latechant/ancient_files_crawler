鷸蚌相爭

注〇漁翁得利。

此亦古人警世之良言，繪者特列旁觀人四，以代漁人耳。其二爲外人，固甚樂觀南北之疲於爭持，以收漁人之利者。其他二人，則一爲舊時積弊，一爲新派清廉也。

THE BIRD AND THE SHELL-FISH.

鷸蚌相爭，漁人得利。

February 3rd, 1912.

A Bird, perceiving an open Shell-Fish on the beach, inserted his bill for the purpose of appeasing his hunger. The Shell-Fish, punctured to the quick, closed his shell with a snap and the Bird was caught. This rendered both parties helpless and they became the easy prey of some covetous fishermen.

Chinese Fable, antedating Aesop

THE BIRD AND THE SHELL FISH is another of those old-world lessons so aptly conveyed either by the word of the story-teller or the pencil of the draughtsman. A would-be oyster catcher is caught himself, and two pairs of onlookers watch the struggle with the keenest interest. Nothing would have delighted the two foreigners more than that the contest should end in the utter exhaustion of the North and South represented respectively by the oyster and the bird. The other pair, hardly less interested, represent the old-established corruption and the newly formed desire for integrity.
