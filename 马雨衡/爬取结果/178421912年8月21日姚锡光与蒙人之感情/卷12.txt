盟誓

四月十號，袁總統在北京參議院行盟誓之禮，除外交團之外，各界集會在場，以觀盛典。

BEFORE AND ON BEHALF OF THE PEOPLE.

March 16th, 1912.

On the 10th March, President Yuan Shih-k'ai, in the presence of a brilliant assembly, the only notable absence from which as far as Peking was concerned was that of the Diplomatic Body, took the oath of loyalty to the Republic and was formally installed in the high office to which he had been called.
