靴子已穿上

南北議和將成，將來滿清政府必要退位，皇室則遷至𤍠河。袁氏此次可即穿就靴子，起程辦事。壁上所挂之小畫，即指袁氏解職時之上諭云“袁世凱因有脚疾而撤任”，惟今日則全愈無恙矣。

THE BOOT ON THE OTHER LEG.

January 20th, 1912.

YUAN SHIH-K'AI, sol.: — "I really do believe that rheumatic g'out has gone."

As the negociations between North and South progressed it became clearer that the Manchu monarchy must go; it was strongly urged that the Court should retire to Jehol, and it was generally believed that this arrangement would eventually be adopted. It was by this time fully accepted that Yuan Shih-k'ai, in working for peace, was urging the abdication of the Imperial House. Yuan had been dismissed at the beginning of 1919 on the ostensible ground that he was suffering from rheumatic gout. When there was talk of his recall in September 1910, the cartoon shown as hanging on the wall in this picture was published. It shews Yuan awaiting the physician's verdict. The present cartoon shews him fully recovered and able to undertake the most strenuous tasks for the nation, when the Imperial House should have abdicated.
