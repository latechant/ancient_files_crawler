劇場熱鬧

正演最新最悦目之戲文，滿洲丑脚挈其發笑之龍，身服袍褂，登場爲人呵叱，令其速退。該丑知幾，即自退去。

THE NATIONAL THEATRE

END OF PANTOMIME RUN.

THIS HOUSE IS UNDER ENTIRELY NEW MANAGEMENT AND WITH AN ABUNDANCE OF CAPITAL. RAISED ON ITS VALUABLE, UNDEVELOPED RESOURCES. IT IS PROPOSED TO ERECT A STRUCTURE AS A REINFORCED CONCRETE EXAMPLE WITH A BEAUTY ALL ITS OWN.

THE NEW MANAGEMENT

BEGS TO ANNOUNCE A RETURN TO THE SERIOUS DRAMA. FEELING CONFIDENT OF THE SUPPORT OF ALL WHO ARE WEARIED OF PANTOMIME & COMIC OPERA.

THE BEST OF WESTERN WORKS

WILL BE ADAPTED AND PRODUCED FOR THE EDIFICATION OF ITS EASTERN PATRONS.

BY ORDER OF THE LESSEE & MANAGER

YUAN SHIH-K'AI.

April 6th, 1912.

THE NATIONAL THEATRE displays its new and most attractive programme, and the Manchu comedian with his pantomime dragon and dress is admonished to "Come off! " — which he very wisely did.
