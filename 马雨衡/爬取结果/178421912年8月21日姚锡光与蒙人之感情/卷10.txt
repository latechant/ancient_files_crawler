此爲重要問題。新中華原用陰曆乎？抑取法開化較世遲千百載，曆日較世後一旬餘之俄人乎？抑即竟從太陽曆數，與世大同乎？余喜中華之果能自擇無錯也，故設此問。雖數月之後，蒙古改遵俄曆，然其必爲勢力所迫脅也無疑。

"THE GREGORIAN CALENDAR (NEW STYLE) MOST POPULAR USED BY ALL UP-TO-DATE COUNTRIES CONTAINS METHOD OF SETTLING EASTER DIFFICULTIES."

POPE GREGORY XIII (redivivus): — No, you can't do better; our calendars are used nearly everywhere.

March 2nd, 1912.

THE GREGORIAN CALENDAR. Here was a question of some importance. Should New China retain her old Lunar calendar, should she fall into line with Russia who is nearly a fortnight behind the rest of the world in time as she is centuries in development, or should she adopt at once the Gregorian reckoning, and so put herself in this matter on a level with the leading nations of the earth.

To ask the question was to answer it, and China chose correctly for herself, though months after it was reported that Mongolia, under pressure, of course, had adopted the Russian calendar.
