IN THE BALANCE.

March 23rd, 1912.

IN THE BALANCE was one of those simple pictures which catch and "fix" forever the essence of a great controversy. And great it was, for this at one time threatened almost to tear asunder the ranks of the reformers, so keenly were the various claims of different cities upheld when the question of the future capital was being considered. Nanking had many supporters for various reasons. Even Wuchang had advocates. But as the artist has hinted, the weight of the argument told in favour of Peking, and so Peking remains still in its pride of place.
