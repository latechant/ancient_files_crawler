機新械利

注〇西方先生云：敬挈機械先生介紹於君。

中華民國苟能建設穩固，利用西方最新機械，以擴張其種種之大事業，則其結果之佳，舉世不難窺測。請閲去年海關結報，税收有增無减，足徵雖逢時事多艱之秋，而效果固不出預料也。

MR. WESTERN: — "Allow me to introduce my friend Mr. Plant."

April 27th, 1912.

MR. WESTERN introduces MR. PLANT with all his efficiency and up-to-date appliances. The world was not slow to see what, in the event of the complete establishment of the Republic, would be the result with regard to the introduction of machinery for the vast development that was expected.

The increase in the Customs' returns as shown at the end of the year proved conclusively that, notwithstanding the many difficulties of the time, this forecast was perfectly correct.
