THE SUBSTANCE, or THE SHADOW?

January 27th, 1912.

A Dog, crossing a bridge over a stream — which is named "Re-Action" — with a piece of flesh in his mouth, saw his reflection in the water and took it for another Dog, with a piece of flesh double his own in size. He therefore let go his own and attacked the other Dog. He thus lost everything, because the stream swept him away.

Ancient Fable.

THE SUBSTANCE OR THE SHADOW conveyed a warning. There was a danger at the time lest the revolutionists — torn by conflicting desires, and divided between the extremes of progress and conservatism — should forget that their one great safeguard was, first the establishment of a suitable constitution, and next a strict obedience to its decrees.
